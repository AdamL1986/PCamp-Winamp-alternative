PCAmp is a fast, good-looking music player for Windows built for people who care about how their music sounds and how their library is organised.

Stream your music around your home. Run PCAmp on two or more PCs on the same Wi-Fi or network and they find each other automatically. Press NETWORK to browse and play the music (and videos) on your other computers, and let them play yours. Keep your whole collection on one main PC, leave it running as your music server, and stream it to every other PC in the house. Set a password to keep your library private, or leave it empty to share with anyone on your network.

Play your files through the Windows mixer, or switch to WASAPI exclusive mode for bit-perfect output at up to 32-bit, with the sample rate and bit depth shown on screen. A 31-band equalizer with presets lets you shape the sound.

The visualizer comes in around 15 styles, from bars, scope and radial to starfield, tunnel and plasma, plus a MilkDrop-style flow engine for swirling, morphing visuals. Make your own by dropping a simple text file into the Visualizers folder (press O to open it).

Build playlists by dragging tracks in, organise your library into folders, and save playlists as .m3u8 files. Edit tags for one track or a whole folder, and use DupFix to find duplicates before sending them to the Recycle Bin.

Choose from seven built-in themes or create your own from a template file. PCAmp can minimise to the tray, start with Windows, and respond to your media keys.
